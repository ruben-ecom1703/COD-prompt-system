## Prompt d'adaptation — Statique 1

**[REFERENCE IMAGE 1]** = la statique hongroise fournie (homme + cartons devant garage, maison américaine, crépuscule).

**Prompt :**

> Recreate the exact same scene and composition as [REFERENCE IMAGE 1], but replace the American suburban house setting with a typical Slovak village house setting. Keep the same lighting (dusk/twilight, warm garage light), the same man carrying a cardboard box, and the same pile of unmarked cardboard boxes on the ground.
>
> SETTING CHANGES:
> - Replace the American vinyl-siding house with a typical Slovak rural house: white or light-colored stucco/plaster walls, clay or concrete tile roof (brown/terracotta), small front yard with a metal or wooden fence and gate, concrete walkway, no attached garage — instead the man is carrying boxes toward an open detached garage or storage shed (kôlňa) next to the house.
> - The street should look like a quiet Slovak village street: narrow asphalt or gravel, no sidewalk curb, possibly a neighbor's house visible with similar architecture (stucco walls, tile roofs, small gardens).
> - No American elements: no vinyl siding, no wide driveway, no attached double garage, no manicured lawn. Slovak villages have smaller, older properties with practical gardens, concrete yards, and functional outbuildings.
>
> KEEP IDENTICAL:
> - The dusk/twilight blue sky with warm artificial light from the garage/shed
> - The man in dark clothing carrying a cardboard box, mid-stride
> - Multiple unmarked cardboard boxes stacked on the ground near the storage area
> - The secretive, quiet mood — a man doing something purposeful after hours
> - Photo style, natural grain, realistic lighting. Shot from across the street as if a neighbor is watching.
>
> Style: Photorealistic, documentary feel, natural light + single warm artificial light source. Slight film grain. European rural realism. 4:5 aspect ratio.
